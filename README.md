## 🎮 About the Game

ZENONIA is a single-player 2D action role-playing game developed by Gamevil. The original version launched in South Korea on August 28, 2008, followed by international releases in 2009. The modern ZENONIA 1 edition was released for Steam on August 31, 2026, with Com2uS Holdings and CFK Co., Ltd. credited as developers.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ZENONIA 1**. This trainer enhances the real-time RPG experience by modifying Regret's health, SP, gold, experience, attributes, skills, equipment management, hunger, and inventory weight. It is designed for the single-player campaign, including class progression, quests, alignment choices, exploration, and combat.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to replay ZENONIA 1 with a more relaxed progression curve, experiment with Warrior, Paladin, and Assassin builds, reduce grinding, test skills and equipment, or focus on the story and alignment choices.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

ZENONIA 1 uses real-time character movement through towns, fields, caves, and other areas. Use the trainer's movement-related options only when needed for convenience, and disable them before entering scripted events or changing areas to reduce the risk of positioning issues.

### Tools & Inventory

Gold, food, healing items, equipment, and quest objects are central to progression. Unlimited consumables and no weight penalty are useful during long exploration sessions, while equipment and attribute options should be changed carefully. Save before modifying inventory or character progression.

### World & Environment

Regret travels through connected areas, accepts main and side quests, fights monsters, shops for supplies, and makes choices that influence the story's good-or-evil alignment. Trainer options are best used to reduce repeated farming while leaving quest progression and story decisions to the player.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat takes place in real time with normal attacks, class skills, active abilities, defensive stats, HP, and SP. Unlimited HP and SP support experimentation, while damage and defense options make difficult encounters more manageable. Disable combat enhancements when you want the original class balance and boss difficulty.
